Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How do I earn money on writernet.org?

You can earn by watching our YouTube videos (each pays between $0.15 and $3.00 — the $3.00 is the highest and one-time only), opening partner links ($2 each), and completing short tasks ($0.05–$0.20 per correct answer). Visit the Earnings page to get started. Withdrawals are available once your balance reaches $50 or more.

What is the highest video reward?

The highest single video reward is $3.00, and only one video offers this amount. Other videos pay $0.98, $0.54, $1.20, and smaller amounts for shorts. You must click "Watch" first, then "Claim" — only then does your balance increase.

How much can I earn from tasks?

Each task pays a small amount between $0.05 and $0.20 per correct answer. The total from all tasks is deliberately kept well below $50, so you still need to combine videos, links, and tasks to reach the withdrawal threshold.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Withdrawals are only available when your balance reaches $50.00 or above. Contact support via WhatsApp or email to request a withdrawal once you hit the threshold.

How do I upgrade to a premium plan?

Go to the Plans page, choose Monthly or Annual, and pay securely via PayPal to vainqueuragevi@gmail.com. The owner is notified after payment to activate your access. Monthly plans activate within 24 hours; annual plans receive immediate access.

Can I work with clients outside my country?

Yes! writernet.org is a global platform. Our payment methods support international transactions.
